# PR #168 mode picker mapping (knispo)

**Origin:** Grok 4.6 (Cursor) · 2026-10-15 · knispo mode picker

Dark means Graphite. Light means Earth light. System picks between
those two from the OS. Default is Dark (Graphite). Earth dark, Loam,
and Mycelium stay color skins. The status-bar toggle swaps Graphite
and Earth light. Graphite is not a pinning slug.
