Top small title "rhizome / identity studies". No paragraphs or fake UI.

Use a pale mint-white core, vibrant restrained jade satellites, much more legible spacing, moderately thinner connectors, deep evergreen squircle. Gentle ceramic relief, restrained polished edges, no glow. Recognizable lineage.
02 ROOTWORK: a new very simple custom lower-case r emblem formed by a continuous branching root shape. Cream-white symbol with jade branch terminals on an evergreen squircle. Must read as a single memorable silhouette at small size, not as a detailed illustration. Quiet premium desktop software character.
03 THREAD: one folded flowing ribbon that bends into an abstract connected root / r with two leaflike terminals, flat pale celadon on charcoal green squircle. Geometric and confident, few curves, no knots of fine detail. This is the more adventurous direction.
Composition: orderly generous spacing, accurate small repeats, large icons dominate each column, neutral board, meticulous visual design. Icon silhouettes fully within frames. Colors from existing forest/mint family. No robots, brains, sparkles, stock atom logo, macOS screenshot, 3D scene or extra decorative objects.
