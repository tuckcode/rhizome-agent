# Rhizome Agent window: accessibility pass (2026-10-10)

**Who and why:** A developer running Prime against a local vault. They type a request, watch tools run in the thread, stop the run or open the note it found, and keep going. They often stay on the keyboard all day, and some use a screen reader, zoom, or the light theme.

- **Source:** `rhizome-agent-design-system/rhizome-agent-desktop-ui.html` (main focus: Frame A, Working, 1440×900; Frames B–F were also checked for the note split, save toast, no-vault, first-run and session list states). The source mock was not modified.
- **Judged against:** WCAG 2.2 AA plus `DESIGN.md` / `colors_and_type.css` (focus ring 2px `--accent`, offset 1–2px; `--state-selected` for selected rows; one mint accent; reduced-motion rules).
- **Method:** I served the folder locally and rendered it in headless Chromium (Playwright). I ran axe-core 4.x (wcag2a/aa, 21a/aa, 22aa, best-practice) on every frame in Mycelium and Ledger. I computed contrast for every visible text node against its real composited background, taking the worst stop of each gradient. I also tabbed through Frames A, B and F, measured target sizes, listed text under 11px, checked computed animation styles under emulated `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, and did a 720px reflow check (about 200% zoom). Raw results: `screens/axe-and-checks-results.json`.

**Totals:** 5 blockers · 11 worth fixing · 13 minor

| Rule | Mycelium | Ledger | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| color-contrast (serious) | 13–19 nodes/frame | 45–59 nodes/frame | Ledger `--muted` fails on every surface, and Mycelium `--muted` on `--surface` is 4.45. axe also left about 23 nodes in Frame A as "needs review" because of gradients and the overlay. My own composited check covers them (below). |

axe can't catch the biggest problems here: the missing live region, the model chip that can't be operated, and the composer disabling itself. Those came from the manual pass.

## What passes
- Every icon-only button has an accessible name: New chat, Chat, Vault, Settings (`aria-label` + `title`), Close note (`aria-label`), and Stop (`aria-label="Stop agent"` contains the visible "Stop", so 2.5.3 holds).
- The traffic lights, brand mark, tool icons, agent mark and annotation layer are `aria-hidden` and not focusable. If the shipping Tauri build draws its own window controls, they must become real named buttons.
- Every control shows a 2px `--accent` focus outline (11.9:1 on `--bg` dark, 5.5:1 light). The one exception is the textarea (W2).
- Tab order follows reading order: titlebar, rail, transcript, composer.
- Every target is at least 24×24. The smallest are `.btn-sm` at 28px tall and the thinking summary at 30px.
- Reduced motion works. Under `reduce`, the pulse, caret and spinner all compute to `animation-name: none`, and the button press transform is removed.
- Status dots and timeline markers always come with text (Running tools · 2, Idle, DONE, PENDING, locked).
- At 720px wide there's no horizontal scroll and the content reflows.

## Blockers

### B1 · Streaming and tool progress are silent to screen readers (names and roles)
- **Where:** Frame A transcript (`.transcript-wrap`), the titlebar status chip "Running tools · 2", the tool card `.status` ("pending" → "done"), the composer footer "Working · last tool get_note", and "Prime · streaming".
- **Evidence:** In the Frame A window, nothing has `role="log"`, `role="status"` or `aria-live`. The only `aria-busy="true"` is on the composer. A screen reader user who sends a message hears nothing until they go looking: no tool start, no tool finish, no "response complete", no "stopped". This fails WCAG 4.1.3 Status Messages.
- **Fix:** Give the transcript `role="log"` with `aria-live="polite"` and `aria-relevant="additions"`. Append whole paragraphs or messages to it, never single tokens. Add one visually hidden `<div role="status">` mounted empty at startup, and write short, throttled updates into it: "Prime is running get_note", "get_note done", "Response complete", "Stopped". Put `aria-busy="true"` on the message being streamed and clear it when the message finishes, instead of putting it on the composer.

### B2 · Ledger `--muted` (#77806E) fails on every Ledger surface (contrast)
- **Where (Ledger):** In Frame A, the subhead telemetry (sess_, model, vault, stop · new), the message meta "You · 14:02" and "Prime · streaming", the "Running tools · 2" chip, the composer chips (model, Skills, Vault tools on), the thinking summary and body, the tool paths, and the ghost Open button. In other frames, Regenerate/Copy/Fork (C), the empty-state body at 13px (D, F), the note path and Save (B), session meta (F), and the onboarding lead and footnote (E).
- **Evidence:** 3.62:1 on `--bg` #EFF1EC, 3.86:1 on `--surface` #F7F8F5, 4.00:1 on `--surface-2`, 3.45:1 on `--tool-bg` #E9ECE5. All of it is 10–13px, which needs 4.5:1. axe `color-contrast` reports 45–59 nodes per Ledger frame.
- **Fix:** In `[data-scheme="ledger"]`, set `--muted` to the existing `--fg-tertiary` #616A59 (4.97 on `--bg`, 5.30 on `--surface`, 4.74 on `--tool-bg`, 5.49 on `--surface-2`), or switch these selectors to `var(--fg-tertiary)`. On tinted danger surfaces (Retry inside `.error-inline`), use `--fg-secondary` #4C5546, because `--fg-tertiary` only reaches 4.32 there.

### B3 · `--muted-2` is used for real text in both themes (contrast)
- **Where:** The composer footer status ("Idle · ready", "Degraded · memory off", "Active note in context", "⌘↵ send"), the subhead `kbd` keys (⌘ . ⌘ N), and the textarea placeholder "Message Prime…".
- **Evidence:** Composer footer: 2.90:1 dark (#5C6553 on #151A12) and 2.28:1 Ledger (#97A08D on #E9ECE5). Subhead kbd: 2.90 / 2.55. Placeholder: 3.10 / 2.39. In Frames B–F the footer is the only on-screen place that says the composer state ("Degraded · memory off").
- **Fix:** Don't use `--muted-2` for text. Switch the footer and `kbd` to `--fg-tertiary` (dark 6.45 on `--tool-bg`, Ledger 4.74), and the placeholder to `--muted` in dark (4.76 on `--bg`) and `--fg-tertiary` in Ledger. In DESIGN.md §2, describe "Faint `--muted-2`" as dividers and decoration only, never text.

### B4 · Ledger status colors fail as text (contrast)
- **Where (Ledger):** Tool status "DONE" (Frames A–C), the toast "OK" (C), the Stop button label (A), "locked" and the "MEMORY TOOLS LOCKED" error label (D), and the "No vault" chips and subhead "memory tools locked" (D).
- **Evidence:** `--ok` #38A169 on `--tool-bg` is 2.72:1, and 3.15:1 on the toast. `--danger` #C03D30 on the Stop fill (#E5DBD3) is 3.88:1, 4.45:1 on `--tool-bg`, and 4.07:1 on the error tint. `--warn` #B4552D on `--bg` is 4.32:1. All of this text is 10–12px.
- **Fix:** Add text-safe status tokens for Ledger and keep the current values for dots and fills: `--ok-text: #276E45` (5.17 on `--tool-bg`, 6.00 on `--surface-2`), `--danger-text: #A3352A` (4.97 on the Stop fill, 5.68 on `--tool-bg`), `--warn-text: #9A4524` (5.68 on `--bg`). Point `.status.*`, `.btn-danger`, `.error-inline .label`, `.chip.locked`, `.badge-locked` and `.toast .ok-mark` at them.

### B5 · The model picker chip can't be reached or operated by keyboard (keyboard and focus)
- **Where:** The composer meta row, "xai / grok-4.5 ▾", in every frame.
- **Evidence:** `span.chip[data-od-id="chip-model"]` has no role and no tabindex, and tabbing skips it. The ▾ tells people it opens a picker, but keyboard and screen reader users can't change the model. This fails WCAG 2.1.1 and 4.1.2.
- **Fix:** Make it `<button type="button" class="chip" aria-haspopup="listbox" aria-expanded="false" aria-label="Model: xai / grok-4.5">` with the standard `:focus-visible` ring (2px `--accent`, offset 1px). 24px chip height just meets 2.5.8. Do the same for any other chip that opens something (vault, Skills). If a chip doesn't open anything, remove the ▾.

## Worth fixing

### W1 · The busy composer disables the textarea, so focus drops to the page (keyboard and focus)
- **Where:** Frame A composer, `textarea[disabled][aria-disabled="true"]`.
- **Evidence:** The user is in the textarea when they send. As soon as it becomes `disabled`, focus falls to `<body>`, and the next Tab starts back at the top of the window. Nobody can draft the next message during long runs. `aria-disabled` on a natively disabled element is redundant. DESIGN.md §6 says "Busy state disables input", which is the cause.
- **Fix:** Keep the textarea enabled and focused while Prime works, and queue the message or send it after Stop. Point to the Stop shortcut with `aria-describedby`. If input really has to be blocked, use `readonly` + `aria-disabled="true"` (still focusable) instead of `disabled`. Update the DESIGN.md §6 rule to match.

### W2 · The textarea focus style is not the design-system ring and disappears in forced colors (keyboard and focus)
- **Where:** `.composer textarea:focus` (screenshot: `screens/focus-b-message-prime.png`).
- **Evidence:** It uses `outline: none` with `border-color: var(--accent)` and `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px var(--accent)`. DESIGN.md §2 asks for a 2px solid `--accent` ring offset 1–2px. In Windows forced-colors mode the box-shadow isn't drawn and the outline is gone, so focus becomes invisible (2.4.7).
- **Fix:** `.composer textarea:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--accent); outline-offset: 1px; }`, and drop `outline: none`. Keep the accent border if you like it.

### W3 · The wiki link `[[memory-loop]]` looks like a link but isn't one (keyboard and focus)
- **Where:** Frame A and B assistant prose, `span.wiki`.
- **Evidence:** It's a `<span>` with an underline, so it isn't focusable and has no role. Its underline is `--border-strong`, at 1.66:1 dark and 1.50:1 Ledger, so even the visual cue is faint.
- **Fix:** Use `<a class="wiki" href="rhizome://note/wiki/decisions/memory-loop.md">` (or a `<button>`) with the standard focus ring. Set `text-decoration-color: var(--muted)` in dark (4.76) and `var(--fg-tertiary)` in Ledger.

### W4 · Mycelium `--muted` drops below 4.5:1 on raised surfaces (contrast)
- **Where (dark):** Session meta and "SESSIONS" head (F), note path and Save (B), note section headings (B), onboarding lead at 13px, footnote and "I've logged in, recheck" (E), Retry inside the error (D), the titlebar "Attach vault" (D), and the composer footer `kbd`.
- **Evidence:** `--muted` #79836F is 4.45:1 on `--surface`, 4.14:1 on `--surface-2`, 4.28:1 on the titlebar gradient, and 4.05:1 on the danger tint. It only passes on `--bg` (4.76) and `--tool-bg` (4.66).
- **Fix:** On `--surface` and anything above it, use `--fg-tertiary` #929C86 (6.16 on `--surface`, 5.74 on `--surface-2`, 5.59 on the danger tint). Or raise Mycelium `--muted` to #828C78 (5.03 on `--surface`, 4.68 on `--surface-2`).

### W5 · The composer input boundary is nearly invisible (contrast)
- **Where:** The `.composer textarea` border in every frame.
- **Evidence:** `--border-strong` against the field's `--bg` is 1.66:1 dark and 1.50:1 Ledger. The border is the only thing that shows where the field is, and WCAG 1.4.11 asks for 3:1.
- **Fix:** Add a `--border-input` token at 3:1 or more. `--muted` works: 4.76 dark on `--bg`, 3.62 Ledger. Use it for the textarea border at rest.

### W6 · The selected session row can't be seen (contrast)
- **Where:** Frame F, `.session-item.active` ("Promote vs auto-distill").
- **Evidence:** The selected row gets a `--bg` fill on a `--surface` pane, which is barely different, and a `--border-strong` border at 1.55:1. DESIGN.md §2 says selected rows use `--state-selected` with a stronger border. Even `--state-selected` is only 1.23:1 against `--surface` (1.17 Ledger), so the border has to carry the state.
- **Fix:** `.session-item.active { background: var(--state-selected); border-color: var(--accent); }`. The accent border is 11.08:1 dark and 5.91:1 Ledger. Rail + Send + this border is three mint hits, so either drop the rail's accent fill in this view or use a `--fg-tertiary` border instead (6.16 / 5.30).

### W7 · The session list has no current-item semantics and invalid markup (names and roles)
- **Where:** Frame F `.session-list`.
- **Evidence:** The active session has no `aria-current`. Each `<button>` contains `<div class="s-title">` and `<div class="s-meta">`, but buttons can only hold phrasing content. The list isn't marked up as a list, so screen readers don't announce a count.
- **Fix:** Use `<ul aria-label="Sessions">` with `<li><button aria-current="true">` for the active one. Turn the inner `div`s into `span`s with `display: block`. Read the working status as part of the name, e.g. "Kernel notes draft, working".

### W8 · There's no main landmark, and the window regions are unlabelled (structure)
- **Where:** The whole agent window.
- **Evidence:** axe flags `landmark-one-main` and `region`. The only landmark inside the window is the rail `nav[aria-label="Primary"]`. The transcript and composer are plain divs, and the note pane (B) and session pane (F) are unnamed `<aside>`s.
- **Fix:** Wrap the transcript and composer in `<main aria-label="Chat">`. Make the composer a `<form aria-label="Message Prime">` so Enter and ⌘↵ work natively. Label the panes `aside aria-label="Note: memory-loop.md"` and `aside aria-label="Sessions"`. The titlebar can be a `header`.

### W9 · The transcript has no message structure to navigate by (structure)
- **Where:** `.transcript`, `.msg`, `.tool-stack` (Frames A–D).
- **Evidence:** Messages are bare divs, there are no headings inside the window, and the speaker exists only in 10px meta text. A screen reader user can't jump from message to message or skip a tool stack.
- **Fix:** Make each message `<article aria-labelledby>` with a visually hidden `<h2>` ("You, 14:02" / "Prime, streaming"). Make the tool stack `<ol aria-label="Tool calls">` with one `<li>` per tool card. This pairs with the `role="log"` in B1.

### W10 · Much of the window's text is under 11px (text size)
- **Where:** `.msg-meta` 10px, `.tool-card .status` 10px, `.composer-foot` 10px, `kbd` 10px, `.s-meta` 10px, `.session-head` 10px, `.subhead` 10.5px, the Frame F titlebar chip `style="font-size:10px"`, `.note-bar .tag` 9px, `.anno b` 9.5px, `.rail-hint` 8px.
- **Evidence:** WCAG AA has no minimum text size, but this is the same text that fails contrast in B2, B3 and W4, and uppercase mono at 10px with 0.08em tracking is the hardest text in the window to read. DESIGN.md §3 sets kickers at 10px (`--font-size-xs`), so the system itself allows it.
- **Fix:** Make 11px (`--font-size-sm`) the floor for any text that carries information, including kickers, status, meta and kbd. Either raise `--font-size-xs` to 11px or keep it for decoration only, and update DESIGN.md §3. Remove the inline 10px on the Frame F chip.

### W11 · Some animations loop forever with no way to stop them (motion)
- **Where:** The Frame E ASCII rhizome (new frame every 700ms, indefinitely). In Frame A, the stream caret (1s blink), the working pulses (1.2s) and the spinner while a run is going.
- **Evidence:** Without OS reduced motion turned on, the ASCII art animates indefinitely next to the setup instructions. That fails WCAG 2.2.2 (Level A: auto-starting motion longer than 5 seconds needs pause, stop or hide). The caret blinks for the whole stream, which can easily run past 5 seconds.
- **Fix:** Play the ASCII growth once (about 3.5s) and hold the last frame. Stop the caret blinking after 5s, or show it solid. Keep a single pulse on the titlebar status chip and make the other live dots static.

## Minor

### M1 · Titlebar chips rely on `title` for their meaning (names and roles)
"Laputa" (`title="Attached vault"`) and "get_note" (`title="Last tool"`) are non-focusable spans, so keyboard and screen reader users get a bare word. **Fix:** add a visually hidden prefix ("Vault:", "Last tool:") or make it visible in `--fg-tertiary`.

### M2 · Icon tooltips only appear on mouse hover (keyboard and focus)
The rail and New chat rely on native `title`, which never appears on keyboard focus. "Close note" (B) has no tooltip at all. **Fix:** use a custom tooltip on `:hover` and `:focus-visible` that matches the `aria-label`, can be dismissed with Esc, and stays open while hovered (1.4.13).

### M3 · Symbol glyphs are read out literally (names and roles)
`kbd` ⌘ and ↵ are read as "place of interest sign" and "return symbol". The thinking summary's CSS `::before` "▸" and "▾" is spoken in Chromium, and so is the ▾ in the model chip. **Fix:** `<kbd><abbr title="Command" aria-label="Command">⌘</abbr></kbd>`, or one `aria-label` on the hint ("Command period to stop"). Use `content: "▸" / ""` (empty alt text) or an `aria-hidden` SVG chevron.

### M4 · Live regions are rendered with their content already inside (names and roles)
The Frame C toast (`role="status"`) and the Frame D inline error (`role="alert"`) appear with their text already in them. Screen readers only announce content injected into a region that already exists, and `role="alert"` on a persistent transcript error re-announces every time it re-renders. **Fix:** mount empty toast and status containers at app start and inject the text. Use `role="status"` for the expected "memory tools locked" notice, and keep `alert` for real tool failures.

### M5 · The "CHAT" rail hint is nearly invisible (contrast)
8px, `--muted-2` at 0.7 opacity: 2.06:1 dark, 1.86:1 Ledger. It's `aria-hidden` and repeats the current rail item. **Fix:** remove it, or set it at 11px in `--fg-tertiary` without opacity.

### M6 · Control edges are low-contrast even though the text carries the meaning (contrast)
The Stop border (55% `--danger`) is 2.89 dark / 2.36 Ledger. Ghost button borders are 1.28. Idle tool-timeline marker borders are 1.66 / 1.50. The Ledger `--ok` dot is 2.85 against `--bg`. None of these fail AA because the text identifies each control. **Fix:** give Stop a full `--danger` border, and give ghost buttons `--border-strong` at rest.

### M7 · In Ledger, timeline markers tell states apart by near-identical greens (color-only meaning)
The done marker (#38A169) and the pending marker (#2E6B4F) are 1.94:1 apart and the same hue, and the pulse that also signals pending is gone under reduced motion. The text status covers it, so this passes AA. **Fix:** vary the shape: a check for done, a hollow square for pending, × for error.

### M8 · Under reduced motion, the static spinner looks finished (motion)
Under `reduce`, `.spin` becomes a full closed ring at 0.85 opacity, which reads as "complete". **Fix:** keep the ¾ arc (leave `border-right-color: transparent`) or show "…".

### M9 · The Ledger "Prime" accent chip is right at the limit (contrast)
`--accent` on `--accent-dim` over the composer gradient is 4.50 at the worst stop, a pass with no headroom. The mock-only annotation labels are 3.94 in Ledger. **Fix:** use `--accent-hot` #245741 (5.99) for `.chip.accent-edge` text in Ledger.

### M10 · Harness chrome only: the scheme switcher misuses tabs and each button wears its own theme (keyboard and focus)
`.theme-nav` uses `role="tablist"` and `role="tab"` with no tabpanels and no roving tabindex (6 tab stops, no arrow keys). Each button picks up its own theme's tokens through the bare `[data-scheme]` selector, so the labels sit at Tokyo Night 2.54, Nord 3.19, Everforest 3.51 and Ledger 3.86. The frame tabs are 4.45. **Fix:** use a `radiogroup` or `aria-pressed` buttons, and scope theme tokens to `html[data-scheme=…]`.

### M11 · Heading levels are off (structure)
The note pane title (B) and the first-run title (E) are second `h1`s, and the note's `h2` sections are styled as 11px muted mono kickers. axe flags `heading-order` on the kit `h4`s. **Fix:** make the note title `h2` and its sections `h3`, and style them with classes rather than heading levels.

### M12 · Esc is overloaded as Stop (keyboard and focus)
The footer says "Esc stop", so Esc will collide with closing the model picker, tooltips and popovers. **Fix:** Esc closes the topmost transient element first, and only stops the run when nothing is open and focus is in the composer. ⌘. always stops.

### M13 · At 200% zoom, titlebar status gets cut off (text size)
At 720px (`screens/mycelium-frame-a-reflow-720.png`), the subhead shortcut hints and the "get_note" chip are hidden, and "Running tools · 2" is clipped at the edge. The same information still appears in the composer footer, so this passes 1.4.10. **Fix:** give the status chip priority (`flex-shrink: 0`) and let the vault chip truncate first.
